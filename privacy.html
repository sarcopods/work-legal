Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

1. Introduction

MindfulWork ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

2. Data We Collect

MindfulWork is designed with privacy first. All your personal data — including focus sessions, intervention records, monitored app lists, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (Hive database). We do not upload, transmit, or share this data with any third parties.

We do not require account registration and do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Subscription & Purchases

If you choose to upgrade to MindfulWork Pro, purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscription entitlements. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase-related data (such as subscription status and purchase timestamps) as described in their privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not have access to your payment card details, which are managed entirely by Apple.

4. iOS Shortcuts & App Monitoring

The app intervention feature uses iOS Shortcuts automation to detect when you open monitored apps. This is implemented entirely through Apple's Shortcuts framework and URL scheme. No app usage data is sent to our servers — the monitoring logic runs entirely on your device.

5. Notifications

MindfulWork uses local notifications to alert you when a focus session completes. These notifications are generated on-device and do not involve any external servers. You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or in iOS Settings.

6. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children's Privacy

MindfulWork is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected in an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
